# Safety policy

## Principles

1. The safety layer is **independent of the language model**: deterministic patterns plus a
   cue-combination classifier run on every message before retrieval or generation.
2. The model is **never the sole judge** of risk, and classifier details are never shown to users.
3. Ordinary conflict is **not** abuse. Patterns avoid generic conflict vocabulary; "hard benign"
   cases ("he has never hit me", "আমাকে মারে না") are tested in all three languages.
4. **No invented contacts.** Phone numbers come only from `data/metadata/support_directory.json`,
   each verified with a source URL and date; they appear only for the region the user selects.
   Generated text is scanned and unverified numbers are removed.
5. The country is **never inferred from language**.
6. The assistant is **not a crisis service** and says so.

## Categories and responses

| Category | Level | Response |
|---|---|---|
| immediate danger | urgent | Pre-written message: contact local emergency number, go somewhere safe. No generation. |
| self-harm | urgent | Pre-written supportive message; crisis/emergency contact; invitation to keep talking. No generation. |
| surveillance request | declined | Pre-written decline + redirect to the underlying worry. No generation. |
| manipulation / threat / blackmail request | declined | Pre-written decline + redirect. No generation. |
| physical violence, sexual coercion, threats, stalking | support | Generation with a safety addendum: believe, prioritise safety, no confrontation, **no couples counselling**, specialised support. Output filter removes confrontation/joint-counselling sentences. |
| coercive control, repeated humiliation | caution | Generation with a careful addendum: take seriously without labelling; gently ask whether the user feels safe. |

Pre-written templates exist in English, Bangla and Banglish and are labelled in the UI as
"written in advance, not generated by the model".

A risk disclosed earlier in a conversation keeps at least the `support`/`caution` constraints on
later turns.

## Output checks (all generated text)

Removed sentence-by-sentence (and before streaming display): unverified phone numbers,
diagnostic labels applied to a partner, deterministic relationship predictions, instructions for
covert monitoring, and — when violence was disclosed — confrontation or joint-counselling advice.

## Known limitations

- Pattern coverage is finite; indirect, euphemistic or dialectal descriptions can be missed.
- Current evaluation of the safety layer is **not independent** of its rules (see
  `research/results_log.md`). Independent test sets are required before any performance claim.
- The support directory covers BD, IN, GB and US only and must be re-verified before each release.

## Changing safety behaviour

Every change to `backend/app/safety/` must keep `backend/tests/test_safety.py` passing and add
tests for the new behaviour in all three languages.
