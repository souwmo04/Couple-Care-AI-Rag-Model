# Safety policy

## Principles

1. The safety layer is **independent of the language model**: deterministic patterns, a
   cue-combination classifier and a semantic (embedding) classifier run on every message before
   retrieval or generation.
2. The model is **never the sole judge** of risk, and classifier details are never shown to users.
3. Ordinary conflict is **not** abuse. Patterns avoid generic conflict vocabulary; "hard benign"
   cases ("he has never hit me", "আমাকে মারে না") are tested in all three languages.
4. **No invented contacts.** Phone numbers come only from `data/metadata/support_directory.json`,
   each verified with a source URL and date; they appear only for the region the user selects.
   Generated text is scanned and unverified numbers are removed.
5. The country is **never inferred from language**.
6. The assistant is **not a crisis service** and says so.

## Categories and responses

| Category | Level | Response |
|---|---|---|
| immediate danger | urgent | Pre-written message: contact local emergency number, go somewhere safe. No generation. |
| self-harm | urgent | Pre-written supportive message; crisis/emergency contact; invitation to keep talking. No generation. |
| surveillance request | declined | Pre-written decline + redirect to the underlying worry. No generation. |
| manipulation / threat / blackmail request | declined | Pre-written decline + redirect. No generation. |
| physical violence, sexual coercion, threats, stalking | support | Generation with a safety addendum: believe, prioritise safety, no confrontation, **no couples counselling**, specialised support. Output filter removes confrontation/joint-counselling sentences. |
| coercive control, repeated humiliation | caution | Generation with a careful addendum: take seriously without labelling; gently ask whether the user feels safe. |

Pre-written templates exist in English, Bangla and Banglish and are labelled in the UI as
"written in advance, not generated by the model".

A risk disclosed earlier in a conversation keeps at least the `support`/`caution` constraints on
later turns.

### Semantic classifier (indirect phrasing)

Keyword rules missed every indirect description of danger in evaluation (0/10 dev, 0/5 held-out
test), e.g. "everyone would be better off if I just disappeared". A nearest-prototype classifier on
the multilingual E5 embeddings (`safety/classifier.py`, prototypes in `safety/prototypes.py`) compares
each message with example descriptions of each risk and of ordinary difficulties in English, Bangla and
Banglish. It flags a category when similarity ≥ 0.84 **and** exceeds the best benign similarity by
≥ 0.03 (tuned on dev). Held-out test: 4/5 indirect cases flagged, 1/94 benign messages flagged.

Because its precision is lower than the rules', a **semantic-only** detection produces a `caution`
response — taken seriously, with a gentle direct check on safety (and, for possible self-harm, a
prompt to contact emergency or crisis support) — never an urgent pre-written template. A detection
confirmed by the rules follows the table above. The classifier is inactive if no semantic embedder is loaded.

## Output checks (all generated text)

Removed sentence-by-sentence (and before streaming display): unverified phone numbers,
diagnostic labels applied to a partner, deterministic relationship predictions, instructions for
covert monitoring, and — when violence was disclosed — confrontation or joint-counselling advice.

When any risk was detected (caution and above), sentences that **excuse or soften the behaviour** are
also removed: denying intent ("even if he didn't mean to hurt you"), framing it as love, protection,
worry, insecurity or "the heat of the moment", or as a joke. Negated forms ("this is not out of love") and
first-person wording the user might say ("I didn't mean to…") are kept. Added on 2026-10-15 after both
local models produced such sentences despite the prompt rule (system_v2 / safety_v2); see
`research/results_log.md`.

## Known limitations

- The excuse filter is lexical (EN, plus a few Bangla/Banglish phrases); paraphrased minimisation — e.g. calling
  being hit "physical contact" — is not caught.
- Pattern coverage is finite; the semantic classifier recovers many indirect descriptions but not all,
  and it occasionally flags ordinary messages (handled with a gentle check-in).
- All safety evaluation data is fictional and single-author; the indirect held-out set has only 5 cases.
  Independent, larger, native-speaker-written test sets are required before any performance claim.
- The support directory covers BD, IN, GB and US only and must be re-verified before each release.

## Changing safety behaviour

Every change to `backend/app/safety/` must keep `backend/tests/test_safety.py` passing and add
tests for the new behaviour in all three languages.
