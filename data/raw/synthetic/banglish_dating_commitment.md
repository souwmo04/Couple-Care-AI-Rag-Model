# Relationship serious hobe kina — commitment niye confusion

> SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — CoupleCare AI development er jonno lekha. Eta authoritative advice na, ar professional support er bikolpo na.

## Duijoner speed alada

Onek somoy ekjon commitment, biye ba family ke janano niye ready, kintu onnojon ekhono sure na. Timing alada mane shob somoy feeling alada na, kintu eta niye honest kotha bola dorkar.

## Shorashori jiggesh kora

"Ami bujhte chai tumi amader niye ki bhabcho. Ajkei decision chacchi na; shudhu jante chai tumi kivabe dekhcho." — demand na kore tar perspective jante chaile honest uttor pawar shombhabona beshi.

## Uttor ta shona

Amra onek somoy ja shunte chai shetai shuni. Jodi partner bar bar bole se ready na, ba onek din dhore topic ta eriye jay, tahole sheta shottyi bole dhore nijer ki dorkar sheta bhaba joktishongoto. Opekkha kora ekta valid choice; abar timeline onek alada bole shore asha o valid choice.

## Family r chap

Amader onek family te biye r decision e baba-ma ar attiyo der bhumika thake. Eta support o ane, chap o ane. Age duijon nijeder moddhe ki chan sheta thik kore, tarpor eksathe family ke kivabe janaben sheta thik korle shubidha hoy.

## Shuru te kichu red flag

Khub druto commit korar jonno chap deya, romance er naame extreme hingsha, bondhuder theke alada kore deya, ar "na" ke shomman na kora — egulo kheyal kora dorkar. Egulo kototuku taratari text reply dey tar cheye onek beshi important.
