# Partner kotha shune na? Kivabe bolbo, kivabe shunbo

> SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — CoupleCare AI development er jonno lekha. Eta authoritative advice na, ar professional support er bikolpo na.

## Kotha na shunle keno eto kosto lage

Jokhon mone hoy partner amar kotha shune na, tokhon kosto ta asole topic niye kom, beshi hoy "ami important na" ei feeling theke. Tokhon amra ek kotha bar bar boli, gola chorai, ba chup hoye jai. Er fole onno jon aro defensive hoye jay. Onek somoy duijonei bujhate chay, kintu keu shunar jaygay thake na.

## Shombhabbo karon

Onek karon thakte pare, ar konta shotti seta shudhu apnara duijon e khuje ber korte paren. Kaajer chap ba phone er karone mon onno dike thakte pare. Keu empathy na diye shathe shathe solution dite chay. Keu criticised feel korle ar kotha nite pare na. Abar karo uttor dite ektu somoy lage. Kichu somporke kotha na shuna ekta boro dismissive pattern er ongsho — setao serious bhabe dekha dorkar.

## Bhalo somoy bechhe neya

Duijon jokhon tulonamulok shanto, tara-hura nei, tokhon important kotha bhalo hoy. "Ekhon ki ekta important bishoy niye kotha bola jabe?" — emon jiggesh korle onno jon prepared hote pare. Ekhon na hole, porer ekta specific time thik kore nin.

## Nijer feeling diye bola

"Tumi kokhono amar kotha shuno na" na bole, bola jay: "Kal ami jokhon amar diner kotha bolchilam ar tumi phone dekhchile, tokhon mone hoyeche amar kothar kono dam nei." Ekhane ekta specific muhurto, feeling ar need er kotha ache, kintu kauke dosh deya hoyni.

## Reflective listening

Apnar shunar pala ele, uttor deyar age ja shunlen ta nijer bhashay bolun: "Tahole tumi bolcho, ami bar bar phone dekhay tumi eka feel korecho — thik bujhechi?" Er mane agree kora na; er mane kotha ta pouchheche.

## Ekta example script

"Ami ekta bishoy niye tomar sathe kotha bolte chai. Kichudin dhore, specially shondhay, mone hocche amar kotha thikmoto shuna hocche na. Ami tomake dosh dite chai na — ami chai amra aro kachakachi thaki. Tomar dik theke byapar ta kemon lagche, bolba?"
