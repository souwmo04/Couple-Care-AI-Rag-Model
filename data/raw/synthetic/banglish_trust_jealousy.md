# Sondeh, hingsha ar bisshash

> SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — CoupleCare AI development er jonno lekha. Eta authoritative advice na, ar professional support er bikolpo na.

## Hingsha ekta signal

Hingsha ba jealousy khub shabhabik feeling. Eta harano r bhoy, insecurity, ager kono bisshash-bhongo, ba somporke kono asol poriborton er ingit hote pare. Hingsha hocche bolei proman hoy na je partner kichu bhul korche, abar eta o proman hoy na je apni oyoktik — eta shudhu ekta information ja shanto bhabe bujhe dekha dorkar.

## Ghotona ar bekkhya alada kora

Ja asole ghoteche ("dui shoptaho dhore se rate deri kore message korche") ar apni mone mone je golpo banacchen ("se amar proti interest haracche") — ei duita alada kore likhe dekhun. Ghotona ta duijoner e jana; bekkhya ta onek possibility r ekta. Accuse korar cheye ghotona niye proshno kora beshi kaje dey.

## Lukiye phone check kora keno kharap

Partner er phone lukiye dekha, permission chara location track kora, ba private message pora — kichu na pawa gele o eta bisshash aro bhange. Onek jaygay eta illegal o hote pare. Surveillance shei kotha ta r jayga niye ney, jeta somporker shotti dorkar.

## Hingsha niye kotha bolar example

"Kheyal korechi tumi rate deri kore message korle amar khub oshthir lage. Ami tomake kono dosh dite chai na. Amra ki kotha bolte pari ki hocche, ar ki hole ami ektu nishchinto feel korbo?"

## Jokhon hingsha control hoye jay

Hingshar ojuhate password chawa, kar sathe dekha kora jabe ta thik kore deya, chola-fera r upor nojor rakha, ba onnoder sathe somoy katale shasti deya — egulo bhalobashar lokkhon na. Emon hole bairer support neya joktishongoto.
